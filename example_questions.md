# KIWi — Example Questions

Use these questions to check retrieval and answers against the current `data/Data.csv` collection. The CSV covers study and exam administration, applications and enrolment, CampusPortal workflows, IT services, student support, research, international study, and further education.

## 1. Applications and enrolment

1. How do I apply to a THWS degree programme?
2. Which documents may be required for an application?
3. How can I check the status of my application?
4. What should I do if application documents are missing?
5. How do I complete online enrolment, and how do I know it is complete?
6. How much is the semester contribution, and how can I pay it?
7. Can I accept more than one offer of admission?
8. How can international applicants get support before arriving at THWS?

## 2. Exams and course administration

1. How do I register for an exam in the CampusPortal?
2. What are the exam registration dates for winter semester 2026/27?
3. Can I deregister from an exam, and what is the two-day rule?
4. What should I do if a registered exam is missing from my bookings?
5. What should I do if I registered for the wrong exam?
6. What happens if I miss an exam because of illness?
7. How can I request a review of an exam or correct a missing grade?
8. Where can I find my transcript or enrolment certificate?
9. How do I apply for credit transfer for previous coursework?

## 3. CampusPortal and student accounts

1. What can students do in the CampusPortal?
2. Where can I find my course registrations?
3. How do I activate my student account?
4. Where can I find my THWS login name and initial password?
5. What should I do if I cannot log in to the CampusPortal?
6. How do I update my address or personal details?

## 4. IT services and library

5. What software does the ITSC provide to students?
6. How can I access library resources from home?
7. How long can I borrow a book, and can I renew it?

## 5. Student support and campus life

1. What counselling services are available to THWS students?
2. Where can students get support with financial or social issues?
3. What support is available for students with disabilities or chronic illnesses?
4. What help can a buddy provide to a new international student?
5. What accommodation and canteen information is available?
6. Who can advise me if I am considering leaving my degree programme?

## 6. Study options, research, and further education

## 7. Multiple-source and citation checks

1. Compare the exam registration and deregistration rules for different exam types.
2. Which source explains how to find exam registrations, and which explains how to change one?
3. What sources support the answer about applying to THWS?
4. Which source page should I open for the ITSC VPN instructions?
5. Answer using the relevant source and provide its name and link.

## 8. Missing-information checks

These questions test whether KIWi avoids inventing information that is not present in the indexed data.

1. What is the deadline for the imaginary KIWi Scholarship?
2. Who won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics?
3. What is tomorrow’s weather forecast for Würzburg?
4. What is the personal telephone number of a THWS employee if it is not listed in the data?

**Expected behaviour:** If the retrieved sources do not contain enough evidence, KIWi should say it could not find the information instead of guessing or attaching unrelated sources.

## 9. Language handling

1. How do I register for an exam?
2. Wie melde ich mich zu einer Prüfung an?
3. Wie erreiche ich den ITSC-Helpdesk in Schweinfurt?
4. Was muss ich tun, wenn eine Prüfung im CampusPortal nicht angezeigt wird?

KIWi should answer in the same language as the question when the indexed information supports an answer.

## Testing notes

For each question, check:

- **Retrieval relevance:** Are the retrieved passages about the requested topic?
- **Answer correctness:** Does the answer match the CSV source record?
- **Citation accuracy:** Do cited sources support the answer, and do their links open the relevant page?
- **Abstention:** Does KIWi avoid guessing when the data lacks the answer?
- **Language handling:** Does KIWi respond in the language of the question?

These are proposed examples; verify answers against the current CSV records and linked source pages.
